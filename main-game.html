How to play

Bongsho Bistar is a chain-reaction territory game. Players take turns placing orbs on a grid, and whoever fills the whole board with their color wins.

  • On your first turn, tap any empty tile to claim it.
  • After that, you can only tap tiles you already own.
  • Each tap adds one orb to that tile.
  • A tile explodes once it reaches 4 orbs: it empties out and sends one orb into each neighboring tile (up, down, left, right), capturing those tiles for you.
  • Explosions can chain into more explosions if a neighbor also fills up, so a single move can flip a big chunk of the board.
  • Once every player has moved at least once, anyone whose tiles are wiped out is eliminated.
  • Last player standing — or the only one left with tiles on the board — wins.

Play locally by passing the device around, or use Host / Join above to play online with friends on separate devices.
